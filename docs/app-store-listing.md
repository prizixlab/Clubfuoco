**Name (30):** Club Fuoco

**Subtitle (30):** Barcelona nightlife, unlocked

**Promotional text (170):**
The city's best nights, in one place. Discover where to go tonight, skip the
line with a guestlist spot, book a VIP table, and carry your entry in your pocket.

**Keywords (100):**
nightlife,nightclub,Barcelona,guestlist,VIP table,bottle service,party,club,events,going out,bar,rumba

**Description (<4000):**
Club Fuoco is your key to Barcelona after dark.

Discover the clubs and events worth your night, see what's actually happening
tonight, and get in without the guesswork — a guestlist spot, a VIP table, or a
ticket, all in one app.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Explore the scene — browse venues and tonight's events, with the vibe, music,
  hours, and location for each.
• Get on the list — join a promoter's guestlist in a tap and bring your friends.
• Book the night — reserve a table or VIP with a few taps and pay securely with
  Apple Pay.
• Carry your entry — your door QR lives in the app and in Apple Wallet, ready at
  the door.
• Roll with your crew — plan the night together, invite friends, and keep your
  group in sync.
• Earn your status — climb membership tiers and collect rewards the more you go
  out.

You can explore as a guest — no account needed to browse. Create an account when
you want to book, join a list, or save your spot.

Club Fuoco is for guests 18 and over.

## Fuoco For Promoters — promoter app (com.clubfuoco.promoters)

**Name (30):** Fuoco For Promoters

**Subtitle (30):** Run your guestlist

**Promotional text (170):**
Your whole night, in one app. Build guestlists, track who shows up, check guests
in at the door, and see exactly what you earned.

**Keywords (100):**
promoter,guestlist,nightlife,nightclub,door,check-in,events,VIP table,party,Barcelona,host,list

**Description (<4000):**
Fuoco For Promoters is the tool for nightlife promoters to run their guestlists
end to end.

Create a night, share one invite link, and watch your list fill up. At the door,
check guests in with a tap and see your turnout in real time. When the night's
over, know exactly what you earned.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Build a night — set up a one-off event or a recurring weekly list in minutes.
• Share one link — send a single invite that puts guests on your list; add staff
  links to track who brought whom.
• Work the door — check guests and their plus-ones in live, with a running count.
• Know your numbers — guests, arrivals, check-in rate, and earnings, per night
  and per month.
• Publish public offers — list free guestlists and VIP tables to the Club Fuoco
  community.

Fuoco For Promoters is for approved promoters. Access requires an account.
