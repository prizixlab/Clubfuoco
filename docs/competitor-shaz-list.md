## The model in one line

Free entry to a club **if you arrive before a cutoff time** and say "I'm on
SHAZ LIST" at the door. No app, no QR, no payment for the free tier — the entire
product is *your name on a list plus a deadline*.

Their own three steps: pick events → fill a form → say "SHAZ LIST" at the door.

Revenue does **not** come from the free list. It comes from:
1. **Ticketed events** (headline DJs, Saturdays at the big rooms)
2. **VIP tables** — €400 to €5,000
3. Add-ons: models & bottles, stag/hen, birthdays

## Operational details worth copying or beating

- **Age policy varies by venue, and they surface it per event.** Downtown is
  **21+**; everything else 18+. That's a real filter — a 19-year-old planning a
  Friday needs to know before they travel.
- **Dress code is a first-class field**, with three tiers used consistently:
  *Dress to Impress*, *Well Dressed*, *Smart and Sexy*.
- **Cutoff time is the headline**, not the club name. Every card leads with
  "Free Entrance till HH:MM".
- **Jamboree is the anchor** — free till 02:00, seven nights a week, all year.
- **Venue filter + music-genre filter** on the listing page (15 clubs, ~35 genres).
- Listings run **two months ahead**, so recurring nights are bookable far out.

## Where they're weak

- **No app.** Everything is a web form; the door check is a human reading a name
  off a list. No QR, no scan, no proof of arrival.
- **Ticketing is rented** — Fourvenues and Notikumi. They don't own the payment
  rail, the customer record, or the post-purchase relationship.
- **No attendance signal.** They cannot tell a venue who actually showed up,
  which is exactly what venue billing depends on.
- **Duplicate listings** in their own feed (same event repeated across
  containers), and Saturday Pacha silently disappears after 15 Aug.
- Nothing personal: no history, no membership, no reason to come back to *them*
  rather than to the club.
